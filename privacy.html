Backflow Tester – Privacy Policy

Backflow Tester is a private tool used by Colin Bell Plumbing (Marrickville NSW) to record backflow prevention device tests and print compliance certificates.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use Google Sheets and Google Drive. It uses these only to read and write the owner's own backflow test spreadsheet, client list and test photos.

Where your data goes

All data stays in your own Google account and in your own browser or phone (a saved copy so the app works without signal). The app has no server. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or shared with the developer or any third party.

Sign-in details

Your Google sign-in token is stored on your own device so you stay signed in. You can remove it any time using Sign out in Settings, or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
